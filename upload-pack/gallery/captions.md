# Gallery captions

Upload in numbered order and set **01** as featured. Title, then description.

1. **A live checklist on your HUD**
   Have and need for every item, missing items first. Find lights up every nearby chest holding something you still need, through walls.
2. **Or just screenshot the video**
   A Litematica material list paused in a video, read by the built-in offline OCR. Amounts in the game's own font come through exactly.
3. **Paste the list from the description**
   Stacks, shulker boxes, x64, 1,280, bullets and typos are all understood.
4. **Check it before it counts**
   Every line shows what it matched. Anything the parser is unsure of is marked yellow so you can fix it.
5. **Missing items glow in every chest**
   Open any container and the stacks your list still needs are ringed. Click Track and that container counts toward the list from anywhere.
6. **Every build, one list each**
   Keep several lists, tick things off by hand, edit amounts, and see what's carried and what's in storage.
